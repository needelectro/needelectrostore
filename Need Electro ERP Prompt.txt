# BUILD A COMPLETE ERP SYSTEM FOR NEED ELECTRO

Build a production-ready, scalable, modern ERP and business management system for **NEED ELECTRO — Trusted Electrical & Technological Solutions**.

NEED ELECTRO is a Bangladesh-based technology and electrical business that sells laptops, computers, smartphones, CCTV cameras, networking devices, accessories, electrical products, and provides electrical/technical services.

A major part of the business is **laptop sales through EMI/installment plans**. Therefore, the ERP must have a powerful **POS + Inventory + Accounting + EMI Management + Customer Management** system.

Do NOT build this as a basic CRUD dashboard. Build it as a real commercial ERP that can be used daily by sales staff, inventory managers, accountants, technicians, managers, and administrators.

# 33. IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULES

Implement these rules carefully:

1. A serialized laptop cannot be sold twice.

2. Selling a product automatically decreases stock.

3. Purchasing a product automatically increases stock.

4. Returning a product must correctly update inventory.

5. EMI payment must update the EMI balance and accounting simultaneously.

6. A paid installment cannot accidentally be collected twice.

7. Invoice numbers must be unique.

8. EMI account IDs must be unique.

9. Serial numbers must be unique.

10. Deleted financial transactions should use controlled reversal/void mechanisms rather than silently disappearing.

11. Every important financial action must create an audit log.

12. Stock cannot become negative unless explicitly allowed by an authorized administrator.

13. EMI calculations must be transparent and visible to the customer.

14. Product ownership/payment status must be clearly represented according to the signed agreement and applicable Bangladesh law.
